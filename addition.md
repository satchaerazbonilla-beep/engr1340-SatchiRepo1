<p> positive integers combine two together increasing the number while adding a negative number decreases the number<p>
  
</p> ex: 5+5= 10, 5+(-5)= 0</p>

multiplying two integers takes the quantity of the first number, and duplicates that as many times as the second number calls for

ex: 3 * 4 = 12

when one of the integers is negative the answer will be negative

ex: 3 * -4 = -12

when both of the integers are negative the answer will be positive

ex: -3 * -4 = 12
